About the Journal

Jurnal Terakreditasi SINTA 2

Jurnal Permukiman is a peer-reviewed, open access scholarly journal that publishes original research, reviews, and conceptual papers in the field of human settlements, covering urban and rural areas, the buildings within them, and the facilities and infrastructure that support community life and livelihoods. Jurnal Permukiman is published by the Directorate of Building and Environmental Sanitation Engineering Development, Directorate General of Human Settlements, Ministry of Public Works, Indonesia.
